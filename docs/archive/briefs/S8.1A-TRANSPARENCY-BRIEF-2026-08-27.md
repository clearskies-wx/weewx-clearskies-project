  `open_water_fraction(fine: dict, derivation_geometry) -> np.ndarray[ny, nx]` — for each G1 cell
  (centre `lat_min + j·dlat`, `lon_min + i·dlon`, footprint ± half a cell), the share of fine
  samples with `elev < 0`; vectorised (bin sample coordinates to cell indices with
  `np.floor((lat - (lat_min - dlat/2)) / dlat)`, `np.bincount` of wet and total per cell); cells with
  zero fine samples inside the box margin fall back to the nearest-sample rule and are COUNTED and
  logged (expected 0 with CRM's density). `wet_mean_depth(fine, geometry) -> np.ndarray` — mean of
  `elev < 0` samples per cell (NaN where none).
  `write_ww3_grid_name_files(derivation, out_dir: Path) -> dict[str, Path]` — writes
  `G1_bottom.txt`, `G1_status.txt`, `G1_obstr.txt` from the derivation's arrays, IDLA 1 / IDFM 1

  `%d` for status, `%.3f` for transparency; returns the paths for `WW3StepSpec.input_files`.
