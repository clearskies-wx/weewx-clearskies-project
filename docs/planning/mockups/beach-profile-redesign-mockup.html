Iteration 3 — every element below is the live unified payload; nothing is synthesized:
  • The sand is the published beach. The dry profile landward of the water is the new beachElevation field — the raw signed survey elevations (CUDEM), not an invented berm. The chart shows a short strip of real beach for context and ends; no more “300 ft of beach.”
  • The waterline is the real, tide-aware waterline. The sand meets the water exactly at the published waterlineDistance, computed from today’s tide. Tide is below datum right now, so the waterline sits seaward of the LMSL zero — the dashed still-water line is drawn at the true tide level, with the LMSL datum kept as a faint reference above it.
  • The zones are the published zones. Impact and foam bands are the endpoint’s own surfZones — which, after the unification you approved, anchor exactly to the published break points (impact starts at the outer break to the foot). The reform trough between the two breaks is in the payload too (hover to see it).
  • The double break is real and drawn — today’s two published breaks sit close together (outer at ~222 ft, inner at ~206 ft with the reform trough between), so the two crests draw as a tight pair; on a big-swell day they separate widely.
  • D6 toggle (decision pending): the “Per-break zones” switch previews what a per-break zone list would render — each break carrying its own impact/foam bands instead of one aggregate set. The toggle view is a DEMO derivation, not published data; publishing it for real is the D6 yes/no. Note today’s breaks are so close the two versions nearly coincide — the payoff shows on wide double-break days.
