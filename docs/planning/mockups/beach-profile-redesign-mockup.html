What this iteration changes, per your review:
  • The wave is now the picture. The surface line is generated from the model's shoaling wave-height profile (1.7 ft at 280 ft out → 2.3 ft at the break) with the crest at the outer break scaled to the model's true breaking face (2.9 ft trough-to-crest, spilling). Wavelength shortens as depth drops (shallow-water dispersion at the swell's 14 s period). Snapshot phase is anchored so a wave is breaking at the primary break. Inside the break: decayed surface + whitewater overlay. The double break is drawn: the wave reforms across the trough and peaks again at the inner bar (11 ft out, 2.2 ft face) — both breaks are the 14 s groundswell; today's 5 s windswell never breaks.
  • Nothing right of 0 anymore. The chart ends at the 0 ft shore reference — no invented beach face. Where the true waterline sits (the −297…0 ft flat-shelf question) is under active investigation; when resolved, the card anchors at the real, tide-aware waterline and the sand meets the water there.
  • Zones re-anchored to the real breaks — a preview of the unification you approved: impact bands bracket the actual published break points (54 ft and 11 ft), foam inshore of each. The phantom 77–156 ft zones from the endpoint's tide-zero side model are gone.
  • Still-water line stays visible as the dashed datum so the wave crests measurably stand above it — the crest at the break reaches ≈ +1.9 ft, and the hover readout gives wave height and depth anywhere.
