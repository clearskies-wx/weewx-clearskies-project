# Fishing species research — round 2

## Scope and handling

This record supports the approved one-profile-per-species workbook.  It contains
sources, taxa, limitations, and conservation-screen outcomes; none of that
provenance is copied into the operational workbook.  The workbook contains no
fishing-law or season-legality information.

Profiles use exact-species sources where available.  A generic genus/family
profile is used only for an existing practical label where the available
evidence is genuinely group-level; the source taxon states that limitation.
Pressure and solunar values are bounded forecast inputs, not claims that every
species has a laboratory-derived response coefficient.

## Conservation-screen outcomes

Excluded from the selectable forecast list because their exact source taxon is
classified Vulnerable, Endangered, or Critically Endangered in the research
sources: Atlantic cod, haddock, tautog, weakfish, bluefish, tarpon, hogfish,
California sheephead, thresher shark, bull trout, common carp, and sailfish.

Retained with `near_threatened`: scup, shovelnose guitarfish, copper rockfish,
Atlantic salmon, landlocked salmon, blue shark, swordfish, and Spanish mackerel.
