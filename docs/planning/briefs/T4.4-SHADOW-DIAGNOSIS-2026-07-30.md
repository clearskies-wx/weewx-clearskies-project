## Verdict: **(ii) — geometrically correct. Invariant 3 over-fires.**

The pier structure and the operator-drawn measurement-zone segment (the 32-transect fan) occupy
**non-overlapping alongshore bands** at every approach angle the shadow algorithm tests
(`beach_facing ± 30°`, i.e. 208.0°, 238.0°, 268.0°). The gap is not a rounding/boundary artefact —
it is 27–139 m wide depending on angle — so 0/32 is the physically correct classification for this
config, not a computation error. Marine invariant 3 (`3:structures_configured_implies_shadowed`,
`invariants.py:68`, fired at `transect_handoff.py:553-559`) asserts "≥1 structure configured ⇒ ≥1
transect shadowed" unconditionally; that assertion is too strict for a spot where the configured
structure sits outside the drawn segment's alongshore span.

## Ruled out (checked, not just asserted)

- **u-axis / offshore-tip sign error:** ruled out — the `u` (cross-shore) test independently passes for
  most transects (22–32/32); only the `v` (alongshore) test fails for all 32, and it fails because of a
  genuine ~27–139 m alongshore gap, not a sign flip (both spans are on the correct/positive side of the
  travel axis consistently).
- **`_perpendicular_bearing`'s +90°-only rotation choice (a candidate 180°-ambiguity defect):**
  tested directly — re-ran `compute_spot_transects` with `beach_facing_degrees` flipped 180°
  (58.02° instead of 238.02°). Result: **still 0/32**. A 180° flip changes which side is "seaward" but
  moves both the pier's projected span and the transects' projected span together (same `ref`, same
  formulae), so it does not close the alongshore gap. This is not the cause of 0/32 (though it remains a
  separate, unaddressed representational ambiguity noted in `_perpendicular_bearing`'s own docstring —
  out of scope for this task).
- **Structure-endpoint-only sampling (`_structure_seaward_tip_depth` using only `coords[0]`/`coords[-1]`):**
  irrelevant to this defect — `_compute_shadow_span` (the function that actually produces the v-span used
  in classification) iterates **all 35** coordinate pairs, not just the two endpoints.

## Conclusion for the lead

0/32 is the geometrically correct output of `_compute_shadow_span`/`_in_shadow` on the deployed HB
segment + pier geometry: the operator's drawn measurement-zone segment lies entirely south of the pier's
own footprint, with no alongshore overlap at `beach_facing ± 30°`. No geometry defect was found in
`_compute_shadow_span`, `_in_shadow`, `_project_uv`, or `_wave_travel_unit_vector`.

**Invariant 3 (`invariants.py:68`, `3:structures_configured_implies_shadowed`) needs operator sign-off to
rescope** — asserting "any configured structure must shadow at least one transect of THIS spot's drawn
segment" is too strict when the segment and the structure are legitimately non-adjacent along the shore;
this is a criterion change (architectural trigger 1) and is not made in this task. Recommend the lead
carry this finding to the operator per T4.4's own instruction ("log, don't change the invariant without
operator sign-off").
