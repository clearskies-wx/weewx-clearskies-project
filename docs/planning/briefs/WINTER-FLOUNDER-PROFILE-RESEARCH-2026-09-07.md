# Winter flounder profile research — 2026-09-07

## Disposition

Do not add *Pseudopleuronectes americanus* (winter flounder) to the operational
Fishing matrix. The current global IUCN assessment is Vulnerable, which the
approved Red List rule excludes from selectable profiles.

## Verification

[FishBase](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/Pseudopleuronectes-americanus.html)
identifies the exact species, its Northwest-Atlantic range and 5–143 m depth
range, and links the current IUCN assessment: Vulnerable (VU), assessed 12
February 2021.

The returned research also identified cold-water bottom habitat, daytime
feeding, April–May recreational peak, and running-tide preference, but those
values are not imported because the exact species is excluded.
