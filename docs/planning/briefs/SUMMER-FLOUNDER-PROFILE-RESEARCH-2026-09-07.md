# Summer flounder profile research — 2026-09-07

*Paralichthys dentatus* is an included operational profile with FAO coverage
`21;31` and fishing type `bottom_fishing`.

Applied values: 10–183 m habitat depth; 5–28°C active range with 12–24°C
optimal band; moving incoming/outgoing tide and moderate current preferred;
slack and strong current poor; dawn/dusk preferred and night low activity;
June–September peak and November–March low activity; no conservation flag.

Sources: [FishBase](https://fishbase.se/summary/1338) for exact taxon, range,
depth, habitat, and Least Concern status; [NOAA Fisheries](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/summer-flounder) for species context; [NMFS habitat document](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/3149/noaa_3149_DS1.pdf) for thermal/habitat evidence; and [Tackle the Bay](https://tacklethebay.com/guides/summer-flounder/) plus [Tackle More Fishing](https://tacklemorefishing.com/fluke-guide.html) for permitted sport-fishing tide, pressure, time, and season treatment. Solunar values use the approved bounded profile terms.
