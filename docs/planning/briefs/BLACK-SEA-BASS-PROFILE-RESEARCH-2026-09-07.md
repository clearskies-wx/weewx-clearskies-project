# Black sea bass profile research — 2026-09-07

## Scope

One operational profile for black sea bass (*Centropristis striata*). The
profile records all applicable FAO areas in one field (`21;31`); it is not
split into regional or FAO-specific profiles. Fishing legality is outside this
profile and remains the operator's responsibility.

## Applied profile values

| Field | Value | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| FAO areas | `21;31` | Northwest and western-central Atlantic range. |
| Habitat depth | 6.1–76.2 m | Nearshore structure through winter shelf habitat. |
| Temperature bands | marginal 7–26°C; good 9–24°C; optimal 11–22°C | Species thermal range and movement evidence. |
| Tide/current | preferred `incoming;outgoing;moderate_current`; acceptable `weak_current`; poor `slack;strong_current` | Structure feeding and vertical-presentation evidence. |
| Pressure | sensitivity `0.75`; falling `1.10`; stable `1.00`; rising `0.75` | Species/reef-fish pressure and front-response evidence. |
| Time | preferred `dawn;dusk`; low `night` | Tagged-fish diel behavior and angling evidence. |
| Solunar | major `1.03`; minor `1.01`; active-tide alignment `1.03`; full moon `0.95` | Species-specific moon-phase catch result plus bounded reef-fish evidence. |
| Season | peak May–August; low November–April | Temperature-driven inshore/offshore movement. |
| Conservation flag | null | Least Concern; the operational table flags only Near Threatened. |

## Sources checked

- [NOAA Fisheries: Black Sea Bass](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/black-sea-bass) — identity, range, structured habitat, seasonal movement, and recreational fishery context.
- [NOAA technical habitat document](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/4038) — Atlantic distribution and complex-habitat use.
- [Musick and Mercer, seasonal distribution](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1577/1548-8659%281977%29106%3C12%3ASDOBSB%3E2.0.CO%3B2) — migration, winter depth, temperature tolerance, and seasonal distribution.
- [Bacheler et al., spatial and temporal catches](https://academic.oup.com/mcf/article/7/1/523/7827083) — direct black-sea-bass catch relationships with depth, time of day, bottom temperature, and moon phase.
- [Secor et al., diurnal vertical movements](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ecs2.3616) — daytime activity, nighttime refuge behavior, structure use, and 10–21°C spawning context.
- [NOAA reef-fish lunar-cycle study](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/59593) — bounded, species- and gear-dependent lunar effects in bottom-fished reef species.
- [The Fisherman: Black Sea Bass Tactics](https://www.thefisherman.com/article/a-seasonal-edge-black-sea-bass-tactics/) and [Guidesly black sea bass guide](https://guidesly.com/fishing/fish-species/black-seabass) — permitted practical angling context for seasonal, habitat, and structure-oriented behavior.
- [FishBase profile](https://fishbase.se/summary/361) — current global IUCN status: Least Concern.

## Source limits

The field values are operational sport-fishing guidance, not legal advice,
fishery-management findings, or a claim that every location behaves identically.
Direct black-sea-bass evidence takes priority; broader reef-fish material is
used only where the direct record does not resolve the profile field.
